Why Severe Scabies Is So Hard to Cure

Severe scabies — when the body carries hundreds to thousands of mites — is the kind of scabies that doesn't respond to the usual one-dose treatment. Whole wards or nursing homes can erupt at once. This article integrates the May 2026 NEJM GALE CRUSTED randomized trial (132 adults with severe scabies). The standard regimen is oral ivermectin on Days 0, 7, and 14, combined with 5% permethrin cream applied head-to-toe on Days 0 and 7 and a daily emollient — cure rate at Day 28 is around 82%. The article also covers who in the family needs co-treatment, how to disinfect clothing and bedding, how to handle institutional outbreaks, and Taiwan drug availability.

Disclaimer: This article is for educational reference only and does not replace medical consultation. Scabies must be confirmed in person by a dermatologist (skin scraping under microscopy or dermoscopy), and prescriptions must be issued individually.
