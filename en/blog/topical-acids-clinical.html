RAR α / β / γ × RXR — receptor selectivity and clinical implications

  • 所有 retinoid 透過 RAR(α、β、γ)+ RXR(α、β、γ)的 heterodimer 結合 RARE → 改變基因轉錄。
  • 皮膚上 predominantly expresses RAR-γ(~ 90% epidermal RAR);RAR-α drives systemic-absorption side effects（畸胎、骨、黏膜）。
  • 所以「越能 selectively bind RAR-γ」的分子，越能在皮膚發揮療效而 Reduces systemic toxicity and irritation。

Mechanism of action

  • Normalize follicular keratinization → comedolysis：抑制 K6/K16 hyperproliferative keratin、誘導 K7 表達、降低 corneocyte adhesion
  • Anti-inflammatory：降低 TLR-2 表達、抑制 AP-1 pathway、減少 IL-1α / IL-6 / IL-8、抑制 leukocyte chemotaxis
  • Photoaging reversal：增加 procollagen I/III 合成、抑制 MMP-1/3/9 表達、normalize epidermis
  • Modulation of melanocyte：對 PIH 與 melasma 有 adjuvant 效果（分散 melanosome、加速 turnover)
  • Differentiation：促進 normal keratinocyte differentiation，治療 disorders of cornification

Per-agent topical retinoid details

Tretinoin (1st gen, ATRA)

  • Concentrations:0.025% / 0.05% / 0.1%(cream / gel / microsphere gel)
  • FDA indications:acne vulgaris、photoaging(0.05% emollient cream)
  • Microsphere formulation(Retin-A Micro):porous methylmethacrylate 微球緩釋 → reduces irritation、more photostable、Compatible with concomitant BPO
  • Instability:原料 Tretinoin 對 UV 與氧化敏感 → 晚上用、避免 BPO 直接同時(microsphere 例外）
  • Pregnancy:FDA Cat C（USPI 2025 仍標 Cat C）；臨床全身吸收極低，但人類 case reports 有 retinoid embryopathy → Not recommended during pregnancy

Adapalene (3rd gen)

  • Concentrations:0.1%（美國 OTC since 2016）、0.3%（處方 gel/cream）
  • FDA indications:acne vulgaris(≥ 9 yrs for 0.1%)
  • Key advantages:RAR-β/γ selective、photostable、BPO-compatible(Epiduo = adapalene 0.1% / 0.3% + BPO 2.5% 固定組合）
  • Tolerability significantly superior to tretinoin (meta-analyses including Thiboutot 2008)
  • First-line for retinoid-naïve users and adolescents
  • Pregnancy Category C → still advised to avoid

Tazarotene (3rd gen)

  • Concentrations:0.05% / 0.1% gel/cream；新 0.045% lotion(2019)生物利用度提高、刺激減少
  • FDA indications:acne vulgaris、psoriasis、photoaging(facial fine wrinkles, hyperpigmentation, lentigines)
  • Prodrug → in vivo hydrolyzed to tazarotenic acid (active)
  • 效力高，但highly irritating；對頑固 acne 與 plaque psoriasis 特別有用
  • Pregnancy:Cat X（arotene 結構的 systemic exposure 顯著）→ strict contraception required同 isotretinoin

Trifarotene (4th gen)

  • Concentrations:0.005% cream(Aklief)— 主動成分濃度比前代低 1-2 個 log
  • FDA indications:acne vulgaris(≥ 9 yrs)，包括Trunk (back-acne, chest-acne) — 為 first FDA-approved retinoid for trunk
  • RAR-γ selective (>20× over α/β) → minimal systemic toxicity
  • Tolerability between adapalene and tretinoin
  • Pregnancy Category C → still advised to avoid

病人問「市售 1% retinol 等於多少 tretinoin」？粗略 conversion 是 1% retinol ≈ 0.025% tretinoin 的效力(based on epidermal retinoid receptor activity studies)。但個人差異大。

Bakuchiol（補骨脂酚）

Psoralea corylifolia 萃取物，結構不屬 retinoid 但 in vitro 顯示 retinoid-like gene expression。Dhaliwal 2019 雙盲 trial 顯示與 retinol 0.5% 對 photoaging 效果相近、刺激較低。Pregnancy evidence remains limited，不建議當作「孕期 retinol」直接推薦，但作為敏感肌 / 成本考量替代有用。

Azelaic acid — dual mechanism

Saturated 9-carbon dicarboxylic acid，從 Pityrosporum ovale(Malassezia)代謝產生。

  • Anti-inflammatory:scavenge ROS、降低 KLK5 + cathelicidin → Particularly effective for papulopustular rosacea
  • Tyrosinase inhibition:competitive inhibitor → 對 melasma、PIH 有美白效果（不像 hydroquinone 有 ochronosis 風險）
  • Antimicrobial：對 C. acnes 與某些 Gram(+/-)bactericidal,without resistance development
  • Normalizes keratinization：輕度 comedolytic

Vehicles and concentrations

  • 15% gel (Finacea) — rosacea
  • 20% cream (Skinoren) — acne
  • 15% foam — papulopustular rosacea
  • 10% / 15% concentration in cosmeceuticals(non-Rx)

Key clinical points

  • Pregnancy-safe — FDA Category B（現行 PLLR 可用於孕婦）→ First-line for pregnancy-era acne / melasma / rosacea
  • Acceptable during lactation
  • Safe for Fitzpatrick IV–VI skin (no risk of hypopigmentation rebound)
  • Initial 1–2 weeks may cause stinging or burning; usually self-limited
  • Compatible with most actives (retinoid, AHA, BPO, niacinamide, L-AA)

AHA — α-hydroxy acids

水溶性、親水。臨床效果與 Molecular size、Concentration、pH 三者共同決定：

  • Smaller molecules → deeper penetration(glycolic 76 Da > lactic 90 Da > mandelic 152 Da)
  • Free-acid fraction is maximized when pH ≈ pKa(active form);AHA pKa 約 3.5-3.8，所以多數 OTC 產品 pH 設在 3.5-4.0
  • FDA OTC ceiling：總 AHA ≤ 10%、final pH ≥ 3.5（2005 年 FDA voluntary recommendation）
  • Clinic chemical-peel concentrations: 20%, 35%, 50%, 70% glycolic; lower pH

BHA — Salicylic Acid

  • β-hydroxy acid,Lipophilic(log P ~ 2.26)→ 鑽進 sebum-rich 毛囊發揮 comedolytic
  • Concentrations: 0.5–2% facial OTC, 2% body wash, 5–10% wart, 20–30% chemical peel
  • Mechanism: dissolves intercellular cement, degrades corneodesmosomes, anti-inflammatory (structural similarity to aspirin)
  • Indications: acne (mainly comedonal), verruca, psoriasis (keratolytic adjunct), seborrheic dermatitis
  • Salicylism risk：大面積、高濃度、受損皮膚、嬰幼兒 → tinnitus、hyperventilation、metabolic acidosis;OTC 2% 全臉小範圍極少出問題
  • Pregnancy：低濃度小範圍可；Avoid widespread application；高濃度 chemical peel 不建議

PHA — Polyhydroxy acids

  • Gluconolactone、Lactobionic acid、Galactose
  • Large molecule, shallow penetration, lowest irritation, humectant + antioxidant properties
  • Suitable for sensitive skin, rosacea, and post-procedure barrier impairment
  • Common concentrations 4–10%

Retinoid dermatitis management

  • Peak:Week 2-4;cumulative dose-related。
  • Stepwise management:
    1. Decrease frequency (daily → every other day → twice weekly)
    2. 「Sandwich」technique:moisturizer → retinoid → moisturizer
    3. Switch to soap-free, low-pH syndet cleansers
    4. Reinforce ceramide / cholesterol / FFA (3:1:1) barrier repair
    5. Pause other actives (L-AA, AHA, BHA)
    6. Severe cases: short-course (1 week) low-potency topical corticosteroid
    7. Down-titrate (0.1% → 0.025% / tretinoin → adapalene / adapalene → retinaldehyde)
  • 區分 retinoid dermatitis vs. allergic contact dermatitis（罕見） — 後者廣泛 vesicular、超出塗藥區、patch test 陽性。
Retinoid dermatitis 不等於「過敏」。多數可透過頻率與保濕重建解決，不需停用 retinoid。但需告知病人預期、避免 self-discontinue。

Pearls & references

青少年 acne:Adapalene 0.1% + BPO 2.5% (Epiduo) 是 first-line evidence 最佳的固定組合。Adapalene 0.1% 在美國已 OTC，可降低處方門檻。
男性 acne 軀幹版：Trifarotene 0.005% 是首個 FDA 核准可用於背、胸的外用 retinoid；併用 BPO body wash 強效。
Melasma 三合一(Kligman trio):Hydroquinone 4% + Tretinoin 0.05% + Hydrocortisone（或 fluocinolone）0.01%。Steroid 限期 8-12 週避免 atrophy。Maintenance 改 azelaic 20% + sunscreen。
「早 C 晚 A」雖經典，L-AA 與 retinoid 之間並無 chemical incompatibility（只是 pH 與 irritant additive）。實證中很多人耐受 layered application，個別評估即可。
Photoaging 文件：Tretinoin 0.05% emollient cream(Renova)是唯一 FDA 核准 photoaging 適應症的 tretinoin 劑型。Tazarotene 0.1% cream 也有 photoaging 適應症。
Sunscreen 是任何 retinoid / 酸類療程的Non-negotiable。對 Fitzpatrick IV-VI 與 melasma 病人，建議 SPF ≥ 30、廣譜、含Iron oxides (block visible light / HEV)。

AAD 2024 + NICE NG198 — topical acne summary

AAD 2024 (Reynolds RV et al., JAAD 2024)

  • Strong recommendation: BPO; topical retinoid (tretinoin / adapalene / tazarotene / trifarotene); topical clindamycin; fixed-dose combinations of BPO + retinoid, BPO + clindamycin, retinoid + clindamycin, and triple combo BPO + retinoid + clindamycin.
  • Conditional recommendation: topical clascoterone 1% BID（Winlevi，androgen-receptor inhibitor，男女通用，2020 FDA approved); salicylic acid; azelaic acid; topical minocycline 4% foam (Amzeeq, FDA 2019); topical dapsone 5% / 7.5% gel.
  • Best-practice statement: combine multi-mechanism topicals (e.g. retinoid + BPO ± antibiotic); avoid topical antibiotic monotherapy; combine systemic antibiotics with topical therapy; consider intralesional triamcinolone for nodules.
  • Trifarotene 50 µg/g 列為 retinoid 大類，是第一個獲核准用於軀幹的外用 retinoid，4 代 RAR-γ 選擇性最高。

NICE NG198 (2021, last updated April 2026) — topical therapy

  • 12-week first-line ladder（recommendation 1.5.1）— 全部都是 fixed-dose combinations，不建議 antibiotic 單藥：
    • Adapalene + BPO(any severity)
    • Tretinoin + clindamycin(any severity)
    • BPO + clindamycin(mild-to-moderate only)
    • Adapalene + BPO + oral lymecycline / doxycycline(moderate-to-severe)
    • Azelaic acid + oral lymecycline / doxycycline(moderate-to-severe)
  • BPO monotherapy 可作 alternative，但Topical antibiotic monotherapy is not recommended（resistance concern, 1.5.5）。
  • 對 retinoid / BPO 不耐受者，可考慮 azelaic acid。
  • Pregnancy:NICE 建議避免 retinoid（外用、口服）；azelaic acid 為相對安全選項。
兩大指引一致呼籲：Do not use topical antibiotics as monotherapy。要嘛配 BPO（最佳防 resistance），要嘛配 retinoid。Sarecycline 是新一代 narrow-spectrum 系統性 tetracycline (Seysara, weight-based 1.5 mg/kg， 對 GI flora 影響少），AAD 2024 列為 conditional recommendation。Tetracycline 類療程target ≤ 4 months，避免長期使用。

Retinoid Conversion Pathway & Relative Potency

Retinyl ester (cosmetic) → Retinol → Retinaldehyde → Retinoic acid (active). Each conversion step is rate-limiting and reduces potency by ~10-20×:

  • Retinyl palmitate / acetate: very weak (1×)
  • Retinol: ~ 20× weaker than tretinoin; OTC standard
  • Retinaldehyde: ~ 5-10× weaker than tretinoin; faster onset than retinol
  • Tretinoin: gold standard; full potency
  • Tazarotene 0.1%: ≈ Tretinoin 0.05% (slightly more potent and irritating)

Bakuchiol — "Plant-Based Retinol Alternative"

Meroterpene phenol from Psoralea corylifolia. Activates retinoid-like gene expression without binding RAR directly. Dhaliwal et al. 2019 RCT: 0.5% bakuchiol BID = 0.5% retinol BID for photoaging at 12 weeks, with less irritation. Safe in pregnancy (limited data). Not as well-studied as retinoids overall.

Free acid % depends on pH: only the protonated (free acid) form penetrates well. At pH = pKa, 50% is free acid. Most products formulate at pH 3.5-4 to optimize free acid % while limiting irritation.

Azelaic Acid — Dual Mechanism

  • Tyrosinase inhibitor → anti-pigmentation (melasma, PIH)
  • Antimicrobial (against C. acnes) + anti-inflammatory → acne, rosacea
  • Selective effect on hyperactive melanocytes — doesn't affect normal pigmentation
  • Concentrations: 15% gel (rosacea), 20% cream (acne); pregnancy Category B

Compatibility & Stacking

  • Tretinoin + BPO: Tretinoin oxidatively degraded by BPO → use at different times (morning BPO, night tretinoin) OR use stable formulations (Microsphere tretinoin, Adapalene)
  • Adapalene + BPO: chemically stable → fixed-combo Epiduo / Acnatac approved for same-time use
  • Vitamin C (L-AA) + Retinoid: pH conflict (L-AA pH 3.5, retinoid pH 5-6); separate timing or 30-min wait
  • Niacinamide + L-AA: long-debated; modern data shows compatible at moderate concentrations
  • Multi-acid layering: skip; pick one acid family + one antioxidant

Pregnancy Safety

  • Avoid: oral retinoids (Cat X), topical tretinoin / tazarotene (Cat C-X — limited absorption but theoretical risk), high-strength salicylic acid (> 2%)
  • Acceptable: azelaic acid (B), benzoyl peroxide (C — limited absorption), glycolic acid (limited safety data; small superficial use likely OK), niacinamide, vitamin C
  • FDA's PLLR (2015) replaced letter categories with detailed pregnancy / lactation summaries

Retinoid Dermatitis Management

  • Cause: too high concentration, too frequent, with irritating actives
  • Acute: stop retinoid 2-7 days, mid-strength TCS BID × 5-7 days, gentle moisturizer
  • Re-introduce: every-third-night, "buffer" with moisturizer applied first
  • Switch to gentler retinoid (Adapalene 0.1%, retinaldehyde) if persistent intolerance

AAD 2024 + NICE NG198 — Topical Therapy Synthesis

AAD 2024 (Reynolds et al., JAAD 2024;90:1006)

  • Strong rec: BPO; topical retinoid (tretinoin / adapalene / tazarotene / trifarotene); topical clindamycin; fixed-dose combinations of BPO+retinoid, BPO+clindamycin, retinoid+clindamycin, and triple BPO+retinoid+clindamycin.
  • Conditional rec: clascoterone 1% BID (Winlevi) — first topical androgen-receptor inhibitor, both sexes; salicylic acid; azelaic acid; topical minocycline 4% foam (Amzeeq); dapsone 5% / 7.5% gel.
  • Best-practice: combine multi-mechanism topicals; avoid topical antibiotic monotherapy; pair systemic antibiotics with topicals; intralesional triamcinolone for nodules.
  • Trifarotene 50 µg/g — first FDA-approved retinoid for truncal acne (RAR-γ selective).

NICE NG198 (2021, updated April 2026)

  • 12-week first-line options (1.5.1): adapalene+BPO; tretinoin+clindamycin; BPO+clindamycin (mild-mod only); adapalene+BPO + oral lymecycline / doxycycline (mod-severe); azelaic acid + oral lymecycline / doxycycline (mod-severe).
  • BPO monotherapy as alternative; topical antibiotic monotherapy not recommended.
  • Topical retinoid avoidance in pregnancy; azelaic acid acceptable.
  • Tetracyclines: limit to ≤ 12-16 weeks; sarecycline (Seysara, narrow-spectrum, AAD conditional rec) is an alternative with less GI dysbiosis.
