腳趾甲變形 12 種型態完整圖鑑
從鞋子、腳型、關節到糖尿病

陳翊嘉 醫師 · 整合 Ricardo 2026 JAAD review、Haneke 2024 Skin Appendage Disord、Watjer 2024 BMJ Open、Nix 2010 meta-analysis 等實證
一句話結論：研究顯示超過 9 成的腳趾甲變形不是黴菌，而是鞋子擠壓 + 腳型問題造成的。最常被誤認為灰指甲，但如果腳型沒處理，再怎麼治療都會反覆。從鞋子、腳型、關節、神經、血管到糖尿病都會影響趾甲，這篇把 12 種變形型態跟對應原因一次講清楚。
提醒：本文僅作衛教參考，不可取代面對面看診。個別治療請由皮膚專科或足踝專科醫師依您的狀況評估。

Toenail mechanical disorders: a complete patient guide

Over 90% of toenail dystrophy comes from footwear pressure and foot structural problems — not fungus, as patients often assume. This article reviews the 12 most common toenail changes caused by mechanical trauma, the 4 foot deformities most often involved (Morton's toe, hallux valgus, flat foot, splayfoot), special considerations for patients with diabetes, and Taiwan's National Health Insurance coverage of common treatments. Content is summarized from the 2026 JAAD review by Ricardo et al.
