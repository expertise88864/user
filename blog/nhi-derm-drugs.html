皮膚科常用藥物健保規範完整整理
給付條件、劑量、療程、事前審查 PASS

一句話結論:整理皮膚科常用藥的健保給付條件、劑量與療程,含乾癬生物製劑的 PASI 門檻與事前審查(PASS)、異膚全身性藥物、圓禿 JAK 抑制劑與慢性蕁麻疹 omalizumab(兩者目前健保皆未給付)、化膿性汗腺炎用藥。規範會更新,實際以最新公告為準。

台灣皮膚科常用處方藥的健保規範一次整理。每項列出：適應症、ICD-10 診斷碼、健保給付條件、劑量、療程、事前審查需要與否。從外用類固醇到 Adalimumab 復邁、Dupilumab 杜避炎、Omalizumab 樂無喘、JAK 抑制劑、Tri-Luma 三合一，讓你進診間時能跟醫師討論最適合的選擇。

健保標誌定義

✓ 健保給付 依許可證適應症（通則七）及相關給付條文，可開立健保處方；品項以處方當時的健保用藥品項表為準
▲ 有條件給付 需符合特定診斷 / 嚴重度 / 過去治療歷程，部分需事前審查 (PASS)
✗ 自費 健保不給付，或核准適應症外使用

口服 A 酸 Isotretinoin（Roaccutane / Acnotin / 羅可坦）

項目內容
健保狀態▲ 有條件給付，需事前審查
適應症 / 健保條件健保條文（§13.4）：限皮膚科專科醫師使用，需檢附病人服藥同意書，以及經傳統治療無效之病歷資料或治療前照片，經事前審查核准後使用。臨床上多用於中重度、結節囊腫型、傳統治療效果不佳、易留疤或反覆復發的痤瘡（這些是臨床考量，不是健保條文要件）
劑量初始 0.5 mg/kg/day，可調至 0.5-1 mg/kg/day
療程健保每一療程於 4–6 個月完成，最高總劑量體重 × 100–120 mg/kg；需再次療程須檢附原來與最近照片，重新申請事前審查
給藥口服，隨餐服用增加吸收（高脂食物）
必要監測用藥前抽血：GOT/GPT、TG、Cholesterol、女性 hCG；每月回診 + 第 1, 2, 4, 6 個月追蹤抽血
女性育齡嚴格避孕 — 療程開始前 1 個月 + 整個療程 + 結束後 1 個月，建議使用兩種避孕方式
禁忌懷孕、哺乳、嚴重肝病、高血脂未控制、維生素 A 過量、四環黴素併用

乾癬生物製劑與小分子藥

乾癬生物製劑統一健保條件（依 115/9/22 版給付規定；本節最近修訂 115/5/1）

PASI ≥ 10（或膿疱性等不適用 PASI 者改用 BSA ≥ 10%）+「照光治療 PUVA / nb-UVB 每週 ≥ 2 次達 12 週」+「以下五種傳統治療中至少 二種各使用 ≥ 3 個月仍失敗」：MTX、acitretin、環孢素、apremilast、deucravacitinib（apremilast 與 deucravacitinib 已列入此清單，該款最近修訂 114/6/1）。需事前審查（PASS）通過。12 週評估需達 PASI 25（ustekinumab、risankizumab、bimekizumab 於 16 週評估、risankizumab 須達 PASI 75）才能續用；續用每 6 個月需達 PASI 50（risankizumab 需 PASI 75）。暫緩續用：生物製劑使用 2 年後若 PASI ≤ 10 須暫停。新增藥物：Bimekizumab（115/5/1）；Spesolimab（113/7/1）限全身型急性膿疱性乾癬。注意：「PASI 10／10／10 三 10 標準」中 DLQI ≥ 10 是 BAD/AAD 臨床口訣，並非健保條文的硬性要求。

乾癬性關節炎（周邊關節）：原經事前審查符合 apremilast 給付規定且已接受治療者，apremilast 可視同疾病修飾藥物（DMARDs）第一線藥物（§8.2.4.4，115/8/1）。

JAK 抑制劑：圓禿健保未給付；異膚（upadacitinib、abrocitinib）須符合 §13.17.1 並經事前審查才有給付—

事前審查（PASS）流程說明

許多生物製劑與口服 A 酸需事前審查 PASS(Pre-Authorization Support System)。流程：

  1. 醫師診斷確認：符合適應症 + 嚴重度標準
  2. 記錄過去治療歷程：傳統治療藥物、療程、結果
  3. 必要篩檢：結核(IGRA / X 光）、B 肝、C 肝、HIV、CBC、肝腎、血脂
  4. 填寫 PASS 申請表：醫師上傳到健保署系統
  5. 審查時間：藥品給付規定未訂定審查天數，實際時程依健保署審查作業而定
  6. 核可後：可開立健保處方
  7. 定期重審：每 6-12 個月評估療效是否符合續用標準

常見問題

健保條件未達怎麼辦？可以自費嗎？

可以。所有需要事前審查的藥物若不符合健保條件，都可以自費使用。但使用條件不變（篩檢、追蹤都一樣），只是不由健保支付。

口服 A 酸可以用健保嗎？

符合健保 §13.4 條件（皮膚科專科醫師處方、檢附服藥同意書及傳統治療無效之病歷資料或治療前照片，並經事前審查核准）即可由健保給付；未申請或未通過事前審查者可自費使用，費用依醫療院所而定。不論健保或自費，用藥前檢查、避孕與追蹤的要求都一樣。

異膚生物製劑健保條件嚴格嗎？

是的。Dupilumab 條件需中重度（EASI ≥ 16、紅腫面積 ≥ 30%、IGA 3–4，病灶持續 ≥ 6 個月）+ 照光及至少兩種全身性免疫抑制劑的完整療程（共 6 個月）仍無效；115/6/1 起 lebrikizumab 亦適用相同條件（另限體重 ≥ 40 公斤）。一些病人雖症狀困擾但未達中重度，只能自費。

Adalimumab 復邁的健保適應症有哪些？

復邁（Adalimumab）健保有條件給付：乾癬、乾癬性關節炎、類風濕性關節炎、僵直性脊椎炎、青少年型關節炎、克隆氏症、潰瘍性結腸炎、小兒（2–17 歲）非感染性葡萄膜炎。化膿性汗腺炎目前健保僅給付 secukinumab，adalimumab 用於 HS 須自費。每個適應症的劑量、給付條件都不同。

健保藥物和自費藥物效果有差嗎？

同樣藥物無差別 — 健保跟自費是同一支藥（只是支付方式不同）。但同類別中健保只給付特定品項（例如乾癬的生物製劑，只有給付規定列出的品項才能申請健保），這會影響選擇。

事前審查通過後，效果不好可以換藥嗎？

可以。同疾病內可換另一種生物製劑 — 但通常需第一個藥物已使用足夠療程（如 16-24 週）且效果不達標（例如乾癬 PASI 改善 < 50%)，才能申請換藥審查。

結語

台灣健保對中重度皮膚疾病覆蓋廣 — 從外用藥膏到 Dupilumab、生物製劑、JAK 抑制劑，只要符合條件多有給付。三個關鍵：

  1. 診斷與嚴重度評估：健保條件嚴格，需準確診斷 + 客觀嚴重度分數(PASI、EASI、IGA、UAS7、SALT、Hurley 等）
  2. 傳統治療歷程：多數生物製劑要求 ≥ 2 種傳統療法失敗才能升級
  3. 定期追蹤 + 重審：每 6-12 個月評估療效，維持給付

看診時跟醫師充分討論治療選擇 — 健保 vs 自費 vs 不同藥物，每個選擇都有取捨。本文僅整理通則，實際給付以健保署當下公告為準。本次內容已依衛生福利部中央健康保險署「全民健康保險藥品給付規定」115/9/22（2026-09-22）更新版本校對皮膚科常見藥物章節。

Taiwan NHI Coverage for Common Dermatology Drugs

This article summarizes Taiwan National Health Insurance (NHI) coverage rules, dosing, treatment duration, and pre-authorization (PASS — Pre-Authorization Submission System) requirements for common dermatology prescriptions. Note: NHI conditions are revised several times a year; this reflects the rules as updated 2026-09-22 (115/9/22). Actual coverage subject to NHI Administration's most recent announcement at time of prescription.

Coverage Badge Definitions

  • ✓ NHI covered: Prescribable under NHI within the licensed indication (General Principle 7) and any applicable NHI clause; listing follows the NHI drug list at the time of prescribing
  • ▲ Conditional: Covered only when specific diagnosis / severity / prior treatment criteria met; some require pre-authorization (PASS)
  • ✗ Out-of-pocket: Not NHI-covered, or off-label use

Topical Drugs

  • Topical corticosteroids (all classes): ✓ NHI covered. Standard for inflammatory dermatoses.
  • Topical retinoids (Adapalene 0.1%, Tretinoin 0.025-0.05%): ✓ NHI covered for acne. Out-of-pocket for cosmetic use.
  • Tacrolimus 0.03/0.1% (Protopic), Pimecrolimus 1% (Elidel): ▲ Conditional — second-line for AD ≥ 2 yo with moderate-severe disease over > 30% body surface area (pimecrolimus also for sensitive sites such as face, eyelids and skin folds); not combined with UV therapy.
  • Crisaborole 2% (Staquis): ✗ Out-of-pocket (not covered by NHI). For mild-to-moderate AD (age per product label).
  • Topical Ivermectin 1% (Soolantra): ✗ Out-of-pocket. Effective for papulopustular rosacea.
  • Hydroquinone, Tri-Luma, Ruxolitinib cream: ✗ Out-of-pocket.

Oral Isotretinoin (NHI Section 13.4)

  • NHI conditions (Section 13.4): dermatologist prescription only; signed patient consent form plus records of failed conventional therapy or pre-treatment photos; prior authorization required.
  • Dose: 0.5-1.0 mg/kg/day; NHI maximum 100–120 mg/kg per course, completed within 4–6 months (a further course needs new prior authorization)
  • Pre-screen: pregnancy test (women), CBC, AST/ALT, lipid panel
  • Monitoring: monthly initially, then every 2-3 months
  • Pregnancy ban: throughout treatment + 1 month after; 2 contraceptive methods required

Oral Antibiotics for Acne / Rosacea

  • Doxycycline 100 mg, Minocycline 100 mg: ✓ NHI covered. Use 8-12 weeks for acne; sub-antibiotic dose 40 mg modified-release for rosacea (when available).
  • Sarecycline: ✗ Not available in Taiwan (US-only).

Atopic Dermatitis Biologics & JAK

  • Dupilumab (Dupixent): ▲ NHI conditional for ≥ 6 years of age (Section 13.17.2 for ages 6–11, Section 13.17.1 for ≥ 12), chronic moderate-severe AD failing a full course of standard therapy, with PASS pre-authorization.
  • Upadacitinib (Rinvoq), Abrocitinib (Cibinqo): ▲ NHI conditional for ≥ 12 yo failing standard therapy. PASS required.
  • Lebrikizumab (Ebglyss): ▲ NHI conditional from 2026-06-01 (115/6/1) for ≥ 12 yo weighing ≥ 40 kg, same criteria as dupilumab; PASS required, EASI 50 needed at week 16. Dupilumab, upadacitinib, abrocitinib and lebrikizumab may not be combined.
  • Tralokinumab: ✗ Out-of-pocket if available.

Psoriasis Biologics

NHI key criteria: PASI ≥ 10 (or ≥ 10% body surface area where PASI does not apply) + adequate phototherapy + failure of at least 2 of MTX / acitretin / cyclosporin / apremilast / deucravacitinib, each for ≥ 3 months. DLQI is not an NHI requirement.

  • Adalimumab, Secukinumab, Ixekizumab, Bimekizumab, Guselkumab, Risankizumab, Ustekinumab: all ▲ NHI conditional with PASS
  • Apremilast (Otezla), Deucravacitinib (Sotyktu): ▲ Conditional for adults with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis after adequate phototherapy plus MTX or cyclosporine (or contraindication); PASS required; not combined with biologics, tofacitinib or cyclosporine.

Alopecia Areata JAK

  • Baricitinib, Ritlecitinib: ✗ Not covered by NHI for alopecia areata (NHI baricitinib coverage is limited to rheumatoid arthritis and hospitalized COVID-19); self-pay.

Chronic Spontaneous Urticaria

  • Omalizumab (Xolair): ✗ Not covered by NHI for CSU — Section 6.2.6 covers severe persistent asthma only (2026-09-22 rules), so CSU use is self-pay. Clinically used when UAS7 ≥ 28 despite up to 4× antihistamine for ≥ 4 weeks; 300 mg q4w; up to 600 mg q2w off-label.

Hidradenitis Suppurativa

  • Secukinumab (Cosentyx): ▲ NHI conditional from 2025-07-01 for adults with moderate-severe HS (Hurley 2–3, ≥ 5 inflammatory lesions in ≥ 2 areas) after ≥ 3 months of at least two conventional systemic regimens; PASS required; 300 mg at weeks 0–4, then every 2 weeks.
  • Adalimumab: ✗ Not NHI-covered for HS (self-pay).

PASS Pre-Authorization 7-Step Process

  1. Confirm diagnosis with appropriate ICD-10 code
  2. Document severity (PASI / EASI / SALT / UAS7 / Hurley score)
  3. Document failed prior treatments with dates and dosages
  4. Pre-treatment screening complete (TB, hepatitis, pregnancy test, etc.)
  5. Submit PASS application via NHI online system
  6. Review time is not specified in the NHI drug rules; it depends on NHIA processing
  7. Receive approval; prescribe; reauthorize per coverage period (usually 6-12 months)

Major Out-of-Pocket Drug List

  • Hydroquinone, Tri-Luma: melasma / hyperpigmentation
  • Ruxolitinib cream (Opzelura): vitiligo, AD
  • Soolantra (Ivermectin 1%): rosacea
  • Brimonidine 0.33% gel (Mirvaso): rosacea erythema
  • Finasteride / Dutasteride: AGA (hair loss)
  • Shingrix vaccine: shingles prevention
  • Newer biologics not yet in NHI coverage

Summary

Taiwan NHI provides excellent coverage for common dermatologic prescriptions including topicals, oral antibiotics, isotretinoin, and increasingly biologics with conditional approval. Patients should discuss with dermatologists about whether their disease severity meets NHI criteria, what documentation is needed for PASS, and which out-of-pocket options may offer better outcomes for specific conditions.
