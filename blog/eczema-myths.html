異位性皮膚炎 6 大迷思
含「類固醇恐懼症」完整澄清

異位性皮膚炎（俗稱異膚 / 濕疹）在台灣盛行率約 5-10%，但門診聽到的迷思幾十年來幾乎沒變 —「類固醇是毒」、「會上癮」、「長大會自己好」、「過敏原測一測就根治」⋯⋯ 結果很多病人因為怕藥而拖延治療，皮膚反覆抓壞，生活品質崩盤。本文一次釐清。

提醒 · 異位性皮膚炎是慢性疾病，本文僅供衛教參考。請務必在皮膚科醫師指導下用藥，類固醇強度與用藥區域、年齡、面積都需個別評估。
廣告位

皮膚屏障 — 健康 vs 異膚

關鍵理解：異膚的根本不是「過敏」，是皮膚屏障基因有缺陷(filaggrin)。每天大量保濕（神經醯胺、玻尿酸）修牆是治療基礎，比避開過敏原更重要。

本篇大綱

  1. 迷思 1：類固醇藥膏一定會傷皮膚
  2. 迷思 2：用了類固醇會上癮 / 戒斷症候群
  3. 迷思 3：異膚長大會自己好
  4. 迷思 4：抓癢沒關係，反正不痛
  5. 迷思 5：過敏原檢測就能找到根源
  6. 迷思 6：民俗 / 草本療法比較天然安全

迷思 1：類固醇藥膏會把皮膚弄薄、變黑、變脆

「我聽說類固醇用久了皮膚會變薄變黑，所以醫生開的我都不敢擦。」
短期、依醫囑、按部位選對強度，類固醇是非常安全且有效的第一線治療。

外用類固醇分七級(Class I 最強到 Class VII 最弱）。「皮膚變薄、變色」幾乎只在以下情境發生：

  • 用錯部位：臉、眼周、外陰、皮膚摺處（腋下、鼠蹊）只能用低強度(Class V-VII)。
  • 長期、大面積、不間斷使用強效類固醇(超過 6-8 週）。
  • 不照醫囑自行加量、加頻率。

正確使用方式（會避免大部分副作用):

  • 急性期（發作時）：每天 1-2 次塗在明顯紅腫癢處，連續 7-14 天讓皮膚穩定。
  • 緩解期：改週末療法(weekend therapy)— 一週只擦 2 次在容易復發的部位，可大幅延長緩解期且不會皮膚萎縮。
  • 挑對強度：臉用 hydrocortisone 1% 或 desonide 0.05%（弱效）；四肢可用 betamethasone valerate 0.1%（中強效）；身體厚皮處才考慮高強效。
  • 用量計算 — 指尖單位 FTU：從成人食指尖到第一指節擠出來的藥量（約 0.5g)可塗手掌 2 個的面積。

關鍵觀念 — 「用得夠 + 短期內把發作壓下去」遠比「擦一點點怕怕的、拖很久不好」對皮膚更友善。長期慢性發炎本身就會把皮膚搞得更糟糕。

迷思 2：用了類固醇會上癮、停了會大爆發（類固醇戒斷症候群）

「網路上說有人用類固醇用到戒斷，皮膚紅得像燙傷，所以我堅決不用。」
真正的「類固醇戒斷症候群(TSW)」是少見、有特定條件的併發症，跟「依醫囑短期使用」不會發生。

TSW(Topical Steroid Withdrawal)主要見於：

  • 長期（超過 1 年）連續使用
  • 大量使用中高強度類固醇
  • 未診斷異膚卻自行買來「保養 / 美白」(這是亞洲常見問題）
  • 主要在臉、生殖器等敏感部位

正確診斷下、短期使用、依醫囑減量，是不會產生 TSW 的。但如果你有以下狀況，要回診跟醫師討論：

  • 已經連續每天擦類固醇超過 4-8 週
  • 停藥後 1-2 週出現大範圍紅、灼熱、滲液(超出原本異膚範圍）
  • 「象牙皮膚」(elephant skin)— 皮膚變厚、皺、脫屑像砂紙

替代方案（穩定期使用，不易產生戒斷):

  • 外用免疫抑制劑 — Tacrolimus 軟膏 / Pimecrolimus 乳膏（俗稱普特皮、醫立妥)：無類固醇副作用，可用於臉、眼周。
  • 新一代 PDE4 抑制劑 — Crisaborole（俗稱寇緹斯）：嬰幼兒臉部也能用。
  • 外用 JAK inhibitor — Ruxolitinib cream（2024 起進入台灣）：中重度異膚臉部使用。

迷思 3：異膚長大就會自己好

「等小孩長到 6 歲就自己好了，先撐著吧。」
部分會，但越早控制好越能改善整體預後；放著不管會引發過敏進行曲。

研究顯示約 40-60% 的兒童異膚會在青春期前明顯改善，但：

  • 30-50%會持續到成年甚至終身。
  • 「先觀察」這幾年的代價：反覆抓癢 → 皮膚屏障破損 → 容易感染、過敏進入 → 日後更可能發展成過敏進行曲(atopic march)：異膚 → 食物過敏 → 氣喘 → 過敏性鼻炎。

近年研究強調：越早把皮膚屏障修復好（每天乳液 + 急性期積極治療），越能降低後續其他過敏的發生率。重點不是「等他長大」，而是「現在把屏障保護好」。

迷思 4：抓癢沒關係，反正不痛

「癢就抓嘛，抓一抓就比較舒服。」
抓癢是異膚惡化最重要的單一因素 — 越抓越癢、越癢越抓的惡性循環。

「癢-抓循環(itch-scratch cycle)」的機轉：

  1. 癢 → 抓 → 皮膚屏障破損
  2. 屏障破損 → 過敏原 / 細菌進入 → 釋放 IL-31、IL-4、IL-13 等發炎因子
  3. 發炎因子刺激神經 → 更癢
  4. 抓得更厲害 → 屏障更壞 → 形成苔癬化(lichenification)—皮膚變厚、紋路加深、色素沉澱

怎麼打破循環：

  • 止癢藥膏（局部類固醇 / TCI / Crisaborole）按醫囑使用
  • 口服抗組織胺（尤其睡前）：降低夜間無意識抓癢
  • 冷敷：濕毛巾或保冷劑包紗布敷 5-10 分鐘，效果常勝過藥膏
  • 剪短指甲、戴棉手套(尤其小孩夜間）
  • 大量保濕：含神經醯胺、玻尿酸、Petrolatum 為基底的乳液每天 2 次以上
  • 嚴重族群：口服 / 注射型藥物 — Dupilumab、Tralokinumab、Lebrikizumab(IL-4/13 阻斷劑）與 Upadacitinib、Abrocitinib、Baricitinib（口服 JAK inhibitor）現在已大幅改變治療版圖。

迷思 5：過敏原檢測一驗就能找到根源、避開就根治

「自費抽過敏原檢測 4000 元，把測到陽性的全部避開，異膚就會好。」
IgE 測陽性 ≠ 因果關係。大多數異膚的核心問題是「皮膚屏障」，不是「過敏原」。

常見的誤解：

  • 過敏原檢測測的是致敏化(sensitization)，不是真正過敏。陽性的人不一定吃 / 接觸後會發作。
  • 異膚的根本是filaggrin 基因缺陷 + 皮膚屏障破損 + 第二型免疫反應 — 不是「對某物過敏」。
  • 盲目避開「測到陽性」的食物會造成兒童營養不良、生長遲滯、社交孤立，並未證實對異膚有改善。

哪時候過敏原檢測有意義：

  • 有明確接觸後立即反應(吃花生 30 分鐘內全身蕁麻疹、呼吸困難）
  • 有明顯反覆同部位濕疹（可考慮做接觸性過敏 patch test)
  • 嚴重異膚伴隨多重食物過敏的小孩

處理異膚第一順位是「修復皮膚屏障 + 控制發炎」，而不是「找出過敏原」。

迷思 6：民俗 / 草本 / 天然療法比較安全

「我朋友介紹的這罐草本藥膏沒類固醇，擦了很有效又天然。」
「立刻有效」的非醫療管道藥膏，大多偷加超強效類固醇或重金屬。

食品藥物管理署(TFDA)歷年抽查資料顯示，「天然」、「中草本」、「印度傳統」、「越南草藥」等異膚藥膏中，不少驗出含 clobetasol propionate(最強效 Class I 類固醇）、汞、砷等違法添加。問題在於：

  • 消費者以為「沒類固醇」會大方長期使用，結果用到比醫師處方更強的類固醇，反而更容易出現皮膚萎縮、戒斷
  • 重金屬（汞）會經皮膚吸收進入體內，造成腎、神經損傷
  • 停用後皮膚大幅惡化，因為原本是被掩蓋而非治療

真正「沒類固醇」的選項都是處方藥（Tacrolimus、Crisaborole、JAK inhibitor 等），都需要醫師評估。短期成本看來比較貴，長期下來絕對比反覆惡化、皮膚萎縮、重金屬中毒便宜。

臺灣皮膚科醫學會 2020 異膚診療共識重點

2020 年 TDA 異膚共識（由朱家瑜理事長主持）是台灣最新的本土治療指引，以下是民眾應該知道的關鍵：

  • 盛行率：根據 ISAAC 國際研究，異膚是兒童期最常見的皮膚疾病，全球兒童盛行率約 7.88%。台灣本土研究類似。
  • 診斷標準：採用 Hanifin and Rajka 診斷標準 — 4 項主要（癢、典型外觀分布、慢性反覆、個人 / 家族過敏史）+ 3 項次要（皮膚乾燥、IgE 高等）即可確診。
  • 三線治療架構：
    • 第一線：局部皮質類固醇 + 抗組織胺（止癢）+ 病患衛教（保濕、避免過熱、選對洗劑）
    • 第二線：局部鈣調磷酸酶抑制劑(Tacrolimus 普特皮、Pimecrolimus 醫立妥)+ 短期高劑量全身性類固醇 + 光照療法 + 局部 / 全身抗生素
    • 第三線：全身性免疫調節劑(Cyclosporine、Methotrexate、Azathioprine)+ 生物製劑(Dupilumab 杜避炎)+ JAK 抑制劑(Upadacitinib、Abrocitinib)
  • 中至重度定義：基於SCORAD、EASI、IGA 分數，合併嚴重影響生活品質(DLQI > 10)即視為中重度，可考慮積極治療。
  • 關鍵突破:Dupilumab（2017 上市）、JAK 抑制劑（2021-2022 上市）讓過去無法控制的成年異膚有機會達到「皮膚清除」，健保已有條件給付。

2024 德國 S3 異膚指引重點(Werfel et al., JDDG)

2024 年德國皮膚科醫學會 S3 異膚指引(Werfel 等)是目前最完整的歐洲共識，以下重點:

外用治療 (Part 1)

  • 每天保濕(emollients)是基礎，不論輕重度都需要
  • 冬天用油性、夏天用親水性劑型；洗澡 / 淋浴後立即擦，水溫適中、避免鹼性肥皂
  • 不建議在所有病人都做「過敏原大篩檢」;異位性貼膚試驗(atopy patch test)不在常規診斷內
  • 反覆 / 慢性個案應切片排除皮膚淋巴瘤(尤其成人)
  • 嚴重度評估用 EASI / IGA / SCORAD,不建議用 IgE / TARC / ECP 等血液指標

系統性治療 (Part 2)

  • 口服類固醇：僅短期(< 3 週)用於急性發作(adults 0.5 mg/kg/d、kids 0.2 mg/kg/d)。絕對不可長期使用(↓↓ 強烈反對)
  • Cyclosporine:核可 ≥16 歲；標準劑量 2.5-5 mg/kg/d 分兩次。急性 4-5 mg/kg、維持 2.5-3 mg/kg。長期不超過 1 年(有更好新藥的時代)
  • Dupilumab 已核可至 ≥ 6 個月嬰幼兒。劑量依年齡 / 體重:
    • ≥ 6 月-5 歲：依體重 200-300 mg q4w
    • 6-11 歲(15-60 kg):300 mg loading + 300 mg 第 15 天 → 300 mg q4w;>60 kg 改 q2w
    • 12-17 歲(<60 kg):400 mg loading → 200 mg q2w;>60 kg 600 mg → 300 mg q2w
    • 成人:600 mg loading → 300 mg q2w
  • Tralokinumab(抗 IL-13)≥ 12 歲、成人:600 mg loading → 300 mg q2w。結膜炎發生率較 Dupilumab 低
  • Lebrikizumab(IL-13 中和)2024 上市，維持 q4w 劑量為其優勢
  • JAK 抑制劑(Abrocitinib、Baricitinib、Upadacitinib)使用前須完整篩檢:
    • 結核(IGRA + 胸部 X 光,3 個月內)、B / C 型肝炎、HIV、CBC + 淋巴球分類、肝腎功能、血脂、CPK
    • 禁用閾值:ALC < 500/μL、ANC < 1000/μL、Hb < 8 g/dL
    • VTE 高風險史 → 禁用(class warning,源自 ORAL Surveillance)
  • Off-label 系統性藥(Azathioprine、MTX、MMF):僅在已核可藥物失效或禁忌時考慮
  • 合併症受惠:Dupilumab 同時改善異膚 + 氣喘 / CRSwNP / 結節性癢疹 / 嗜酸性食道炎

結語 — 治療異膚的 4 件事

  1. 每天保濕（神經醯胺乳液，洗澡後 3 分鐘內擦完）：這是最便宜、最有效的治療基礎。
  2. 急性發作期積極治療：該擦類固醇的時候不要拖延，把火滅掉再說。
  3. 緩解期 proactive therapy：好發部位每週 2 次低強度藥膏 / TCI 預防，不要等大爆發才處理。
  4. 中重度族群 — 認識新藥:Dupilumab、JAK inhibitor 等新一代生物製劑與口服藥已大幅改變預後。如果中強度類固醇 + TCI 控制不下來，該與醫師討論升級。

延伸閱讀：痘痘相關迷思看 痘痘 9 大迷思。皮膚屏障壞掉怎麼修？保濕乳液、保濕產品成分指南整理中。

常見問題（PTT / Dcard 上常被問的）

異位性皮膚炎 PTT 推薦的乳液如何挑？

網路常被討論的：理膚寶水 Lipikar AP+M、艾芙美 Avene Xeracalm、Cetaphil RestoraDerm、艾惟諾 Aveeno 燕麥系列、Bioderma Atoderm Intensive。挑選重點：含神經醯胺(ceramide)、膽固醇、脂肪酸 3:1:1的修護型 > 普通保濕。每天大量、厚塗、洗澡後 3 分鐘內擦比挑哪個牌子重要。

異膚多久會好？長大會不會自己好？

40-60% 兒童在青春期前明顯改善；30-50% 持續到成年。重要的是「越早控制好、越能避免後續過敏進行曲（食物過敏 → 氣喘 → 鼻過敏）」。新一代生物製劑(Dupilumab)+JAK inhibitor 已能讓多數中重度成年異膚維持幾乎沒症狀，不再像過去「治不好只能忍」。

異膚飲食真的要忌口嗎？

只在明確時間相關（吃某食物 30 分-2 小時內必發作）+有 IgE 過敏史時才忌口。盲目戒奶蛋海鮮會造成兒童營養不均、社交困擾，且大多無效。「過敏原檢測 100 項套組」陽性≠過敏，不要看到一堆紅字就全戒。

類固醇藥膏可以擦多久？PTT 上很多人說會傷皮膚？

正確使用是非常安全的第一線治療。臉/眼周/外陰用 Class V-VII（弱），四肢可用 Class III-IV（中強），手腳掌可用 Class I-II（強）。急性期每天 1-2 次連續 7-14 天，進入維持期改週末 2 次。長期亂擦最強類固醇才會皮膚萎縮。詳見 本文迷思 1。

異膚健保有給付什麼藥？

健保涵蓋：類固醇藥膏（各強度）、Tacrolimus 普特皮、Pimecrolimus 醫立妥（都需符合條件）、Crisaborole 寇緹斯、口服免疫抑制劑(Cyclosporine)。Dupilumab（杜避炎）、JAK inhibitor 健保有條件給付（嚴重度+傳統治療失敗）。自費 Dupilumab 約 NT$ 16,000-20,000/針，2 週 1 次。

Myth 1: Topical steroids always thin, darken, or weaken the skin

"I heard long-term steroids cause skin thinning and darkening, so I never use what my doctor prescribes."
When used short-term, at the right potency for the right body site, topical corticosteroids are extremely safe and effective first-line therapy.

Topical steroids are graded into 7 classes (Class I strongest, Class VII weakest). Skin thinning or discoloration almost always traces to one of these scenarios:

  • Wrong site, wrong potency: face, eyelids, genitals, and skin folds (axilla, groin) should only see Class V-VII (low-potency).
  • Long-term, large-area, uninterrupted high-potency use (more than 6-8 weeks).
  • Self-escalating dose or frequency outside medical guidance.

Used correctly:

  • Acute flare: 1-2 times daily on inflamed/itchy patches for 7-14 consecutive days to break the cycle.
  • Maintenance: switch to weekend therapy — twice weekly on relapse-prone areas. Markedly extends remission without atrophy.
  • Match potency to site: face → hydrocortisone 1% or desonide 0.05%; limbs → betamethasone valerate 0.1%; thick skin (palms/soles) → high potency.
  • Fingertip Unit (FTU): the amount squeezed from an adult fingertip to first knuckle (~0.5g) covers two adult palms.

Key concept — "Use enough to put the fire out short-term" is far kinder to the skin than "barely use anything for fear of side effects." Chronic uncontrolled inflammation damages the skin far worse than appropriate steroid use.

Myth 2: Topical steroids cause addiction and withdrawal

"I read online that steroid withdrawal can leave skin red like a burn — I'll never use it."
True topical steroid withdrawal (TSW) is uncommon and arises under specific conditions. It does not happen with appropriate short-term medical use.

Per Hajar et al. (JAAD 2015), TSW is mostly seen with:

  • Long-term continuous use over 1 year
  • High-volume use of mid- to high-potency steroids
  • Self-medicated use on undiagnosed conditions (or for "skin lightening" — common in Asia)
  • Sensitive sites: face and genitals

With proper diagnosis, short courses, and tapering — TSW does not develop. But you should return to your doctor if you experience:

  • Daily use beyond 4-8 weeks without medical reassessment
  • Within 1-2 weeks of stopping: widespread redness, burning, oozing beyond your original eczema area
  • "Elephant skin" — thickened, wrinkled, scaly

Steroid-sparing alternatives for maintenance:

  • Topical calcineurin inhibitors — Tacrolimus / Pimecrolimus: no atrophy, suitable for face and eyelids.
  • PDE-4 inhibitor — Crisaborole: usable from 3 months of age.
  • Topical JAK inhibitor — Ruxolitinib cream: moderate-severe AD, ages 12+.

Myth 3: Kids will grow out of eczema

"My child is 6 — let's just wait it out."
Some will, but better early control means better long-term outcomes. Untreated AD drives the atopic march.

Studies show 40-60% of childhood AD improves notably by adolescence, but:

  • 30-50% persist into adulthood, sometimes lifelong.
  • "Just observing" the early years has costs: repeated scratching → barrier damage → easier skin infection and allergen sensitization → progression of the atopic march (eczema → food allergy → asthma → allergic rhinitis).

Recent evidence emphasizes that early restoration of the skin barrier (daily moisturization plus active flare treatment) reduces downstream allergic disease incidence. The point isn't "wait for them to grow up"; it's "protect the barrier now."

Myth 4: Scratching is harmless — it doesn't hurt

"It itches, so I scratch it. It feels better afterwards."
Scratching is the single most important driver of AD worsening — the itch-scratch cycle.

The mechanism:

  1. Itch → scratch → barrier damage
  2. Barrier damage → allergens / bacteria penetrate → release of IL-31, IL-4, IL-13 inflammatory cytokines
  3. Cytokines stimulate nerves → more itch
  4. More vigorous scratching → worse barrier → lichenification (thickened, exaggerated skin lines, hyperpigmentation)

How to break the cycle:

  • Anti-itch topicals (corticosteroids / TCIs / Crisaborole) per medical guidance
  • Oral antihistamines (especially before bed) to reduce nocturnal scratching
  • Cold compress: a wet towel or cool gel pack wrapped in gauze for 5-10 minutes — often more effective than topicals alone
  • Trim nails short, cotton gloves (especially at night for kids)
  • Heavy moisturizing: ceramide / hyaluronic acid / petrolatum-based emollients twice daily or more
  • Severe cases — systemic options: Dupilumab, Tralokinumab, Lebrikizumab (IL-4/13 blockers); Upadacitinib, Abrocitinib, Baricitinib (oral JAK inhibitors). Per the BREEZE-AD and JADE COMPARE trials, these have transformed outcomes in moderate-severe AD.

Myth 5: An allergy test reveals the root cause — avoid the trigger and you're cured

"I paid NT$ 4000 for IgE testing. I'll avoid every food that came back positive and the eczema will resolve."
A positive IgE ≠ causation. The core problem in AD is the skin barrier, not "allergens."

Common misconceptions:

  • Allergy testing detects sensitization, not clinical allergy. A positive doesn't mean a reaction will occur.
  • The root pathology of AD is filaggrin gene defects + barrier dysfunction + Type 2 inflammation, not "allergic to X."
  • Blanket avoidance of foods that test positive can cause malnutrition, growth restriction, and social isolation in children, with no proven AD benefit.

When allergy testing is meaningful:

  • Clear immediate reaction after exposure (e.g., generalized urticaria + breathing difficulty 30 minutes after eating peanuts)
  • Recurrent eczema in the same anatomic location suggesting contact allergy (consider patch testing)
  • Severe eczema in a child with multiple suspected food allergies

For most AD, "repair the barrier and control inflammation" takes precedence over "find the allergen."

Myth 6: Herbal / natural / folk remedies are safer

"My friend recommended this herbal cream — no steroids, all natural, very effective."
"Instantly effective" non-prescription creams frequently contain undeclared super-potent steroids or heavy metals.

Taiwan FDA inspections have repeatedly found that "natural", "Chinese herb", "Ayurvedic", or "Vietnamese folk" eczema creams contain unlabelled clobetasol propionate (Class I, the strongest), mercury, or arsenic. The danger:

  • Patients believe "no steroids" and apply liberally long-term — ironically receiving stronger steroid exposure than any dermatologist would prescribe, with predictable atrophy and withdrawal
  • Mercury and arsenic absorb through skin, causing renal and neurologic damage
  • After stopping, severe rebound — because the original disease was masked, never treated

Genuinely "no steroid" options are all prescription medications (Tacrolimus, Crisaborole, JAK inhibitors) requiring physician evaluation. The short-term cost is higher, but the long-term cost — repeated worsening, atrophy, heavy metal toxicity — is incomparably worse.

Taiwanese Dermatological Association (TDA) 2020 AD Consensus — key points

Per the 2020 TDA AD consensus chaired by Prof. Chia-Yu Chu, key takeaways for patients:

  • Prevalence: AD is the most common skin disease in childhood, with global pediatric prevalence ~7.88% (ISAAC study); Taiwan rates are similar.
  • Diagnosis: Hanifin and Rajka criteria — 4 major (pruritus, typical morphology/distribution, chronic relapsing course, personal/family atopy) plus 3 minor features for diagnosis.
  • Three-tier treatment framework:
    • Tier 1: topical corticosteroids + antihistamines + patient education (moisturization, avoid heat, use gentle cleansers)
    • Tier 2: topical calcineurin inhibitors (Tacrolimus, Pimecrolimus) + short-course systemic steroids + phototherapy + topical/systemic antibiotics
    • Tier 3: systemic immunomodulators (Cyclosporine, Methotrexate, Azathioprine) + biologics (Dupilumab) + JAK inhibitors (Upadacitinib, Abrocitinib)
  • Moderate-to-severe definition: based on SCORAD, EASI, IGA, plus DLQI > 10 indicating significant impact on quality of life.
  • Breakthrough: Dupilumab (FDA-approved 2017) and JAK inhibitors (2021-2022) have given previously uncontrolled adult AD patients a real chance of achieving "skin clearance." Both are conditionally covered by Taiwan's National Health Insurance.

Bottom line — 4 things that matter for AD

  1. Daily moisturization (ceramide-based, applied within 3 minutes of bathing): the cheapest, most effective foundation of treatment.
  2. Active treatment of acute flares: when steroids are indicated, don't delay — put the fire out.
  3. Proactive maintenance therapy: twice-weekly low-potency steroid or TCI on relapse-prone sites — don't wait for the next big flare.
  4. Recognize new options for moderate-severe disease: Dupilumab, JAK inhibitors, and other biologics have transformed outcomes. If mid-potency steroid + TCI isn't controlling things, talk to your dermatologist about escalation.

Frequently asked (PTT / Dcard style)

Which moisturizers are best for AD?

Frequently discussed online: La Roche-Posay Lipikar AP+M, Avène Xeracalm, Cetaphil RestoraDerm, Aveeno (oat-based), Bioderma Atoderm Intensive. The selection criteria: barrier-repair formulas with ceramide / cholesterol / fatty acids in 3:1:1 ratio beat plain moisturizers. Volume, thick application, within 3 minutes of bathing matters more than the specific brand.

How long until AD improves? Will kids grow out of it?

40-60% of children improve markedly by adolescence; 30-50% persist into adulthood. The key insight: "earlier control reduces the atopic march (food allergy → asthma → allergic rhinitis)." Modern biologics (Dupilumab) and JAK inhibitors can keep most moderate-severe adult AD essentially symptom-free — no longer "live with it."

Do I need to avoid certain foods?

Only with clearly time-locked reactions (within 30 min - 2 hours after a specific food) plus a documented IgE-mediated history. Blanket avoidance of dairy, eggs, seafood causes nutrition imbalance and social difficulty in children, usually with no benefit. A 100-panel positive screen ≠ allergy — don't eliminate everything based on red marks on a report.

How long can I use steroid cream?

Used correctly, it's very safe first-line treatment. Face/eyelids/genitals: Class V-VII (low). Limbs: Class III-IV (mid-high). Palms/soles: Class I-II (high). Acute flare: 1-2× daily for 7-14 days; then weekend therapy 2×/week. Atrophy mostly stems from years-long misuse of the strongest steroids on inappropriate sites. See more in Topical Steroids Complete Guide.

What does Taiwan NHI cover?

Covered: topical steroids (all potencies), Tacrolimus (Protopic), Pimecrolimus (Elidel), Crisaborole (Eucrisa, criteria-based), oral immunosuppressants (Cyclosporine). Dupilumab and JAK inhibitors are conditionally covered for moderate-severe disease that failed traditional therapy. Out-of-pocket Dupilumab is roughly NT$ 16,000-22,000 per injection, every 2 weeks.
